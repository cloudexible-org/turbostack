### Added

- **Typed events and identified users for ad measurement (`packages/analytics`, `apps/www`):** `useCapture<Events>()` gives each app a typed `capture`, so an event can't be sent with the wrong name or properties; `apps/www/lib/analytics.ts` is the catalog, starting with `user_signed_up`. `useIdentifyUser` identifies the signed-in Clerk user (id, email, name) and resets on sign-out, so the first-touch UTMs and ad click ids PostHog keeps for an anonymous visitor reach their person profile. A brand-new account (created within 10 minutes of its first identify) also sends `user_signed_up`, the lead event. `AnalyticsIdentity` is mounted only when Clerk is configured. Forks add their own lead and conversion events to the catalog and forward them with PostHog's ads destinations rather than pixels.

### Changed

- **PostHog is relayed through each app's own origin (`apps/www`, `apps/app`, `packages/analytics`):** The browser sends events to `/relay`, which PostHog's region hosts serve through rewrites: `next.config.ts` builds them with `relayRewrites` (`@repo/analytics/hosts`), and `apps/app/vercel.json` holds the same rules by hand, kept in step by a unit test (`apps/app` gains Vitest for it). Ad blockers that block `*.posthog.com` hide a share of visitors and conversions, which skews ad reporting. The rules capture the path with `:path(.*)`, not `:path*`: on Vercel `:path*` doesn't match PostHog's trailing slashes (`/e/`, `/flags/`), so those requests fall through to the SPA and every event POST gets a 405, while static scripts still load. `apps/www` sets `skipTrailingSlashRedirect` and keeps Clerk's proxy off `/relay`; `apps/app` relays only in production builds against US cloud and sends directly otherwise. Checked against a local `next start`: `POST /relay/e/`, `/relay/e`, `/relay/i/v0/e/` and `/relay/flags/` all reach PostHog. The same rules were checked on a Vercel-hosted Vite SPA.
